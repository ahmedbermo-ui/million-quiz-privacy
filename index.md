# Million Quiz privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

## Summary

Million Quiz has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address or phone number. Your games, scores and settings stay on your own device. From version 1.1 the app shows one small advertising banner, supplied by Google AdMob, and lets you report a question to the developer.

## What stays on your device

- Your chosen age group and game language, used to choose suitable questions.
- Your game progress, scores, bank of points, level, streak, achievements, fact collection and chosen avatar.
- The first names or nicknames you type for a family game.
- Your settings (language, theme, text size, motion, haptics, read-aloud, Zen mode).
- Questions you have seen or got wrong, so they are not repeated and can come back for practice.

This data is kept in the app's private storage. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

## Advertising (version 1.1 and later)

The question screen shows one small banner ad from Google AdMob, in its own grey strip above the game. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address and ad interactions. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown; until then only limited, non-personalised ads appear. You can change your choice in the app's Settings. Google's use of this data is described at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Reporting a question (version 1.1 and later)

When you report a question and tap Send, the app sends the question, the reason you chose, anything you typed, and the app version and language to a form owned by the developer. Nothing that identifies you is sent. Please do not type personal information in the note.

## Sharing

When you share a fact card, your million card, your daily result or a question with a friend, the app hands it to your phone's own share menu. It goes only where you choose to send it.

## Reading aloud

If you turn on "Read questions aloud", the text is spoken by your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine.

## What we do not do

- We do not ask you to create an account or give a name, email address or phone number.
- We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
- We do not sell personal information.
- There is no chat and no contact with other players.

## Who the app is for

Million Quiz is intended for people aged 13 and over. It has no chat, no public profiles and no contact with other players.

## Changes

If a later version collects other data (for example to sync progress), this policy will be updated first and the store listing's data safety section will be changed to match.
