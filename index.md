# Million Quiz privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

## Summary

Million Quiz does not collect, send or share any personal information. Everything you do in the app is stored only on your own device.

## What stays on your device

- Your chosen age group and country, used to choose suitable questions.
- Your game progress, scores, level, streak, achievements and chosen avatar.
- Your settings (theme, text size, motion, haptics).
- Questions you have seen, so they are not repeated, and reports you send about a question.

This data is kept in the app's private storage. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

## What we do not do

- We do not ask you to create an account or give a name, email address or phone number.
- We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
- We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking in this version.
- We do not share data with anyone, because we do not receive any.
- The app does not need an internet connection to work.

## Who the app is for

Million Quiz is intended for people aged 13 and over. It has no chat, no public profiles and no contact with other players.

## Changes

If a later version collects any data (for example to download new questions, sync progress, or show ads), this policy will be updated first and the store listing's data safety section will be changed to match.

Questions about this policy: please open an issue at [github.com/ahmedbermo-ui/million-quiz-privacy/issues](https://github.com/ahmedbermo-ui/million-quiz-privacy/issues).
